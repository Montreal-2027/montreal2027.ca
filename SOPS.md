## Change control
All work, both technical and content, with the exception of updates to the Committee and Staff taxonomy done by the Onboarding Team, will be tracked in [GitHub](https://github.com/Montreal-2027). There are 2 projects within the Montréal 2027 organization: [Tech](https://github.com/orgs/Montreal-2027/projects/1) and [Content](https://github.com/orgs/Montreal-2027/projects/2). If you are not familiar with how GitHub operates, reach out to an experienced Web Team member.

1. When you first join the Web Team, go to [GitHub](https://github.com), click on `Sign up` and then `Continue with Google`. Log in using your Montréal 2027 email address. Choose a username, and click `Create account >`.
2. Once your account is created, send your GitHub username to the Area Head so you can be invited to the Web Team on GitHub.
3. When you receive the email with the invitation, click on the link to accept the the invitation and join the team. You will be added to the [Montréal 2027 repository](https://github.com/Montreal-2027/montreal2027.ca) which is where all the configuration, custom templates, styling, and configuration is stored.

## Content revisions, including translations
This procedure applies only to content updates. Content updates may be made by any member of the Web Team.  If you have not received training on how to enter and update content, reach out to the Area Head to schedule training. This site uses features that are not common to Drupal websites that require familiarization. This SOP applies to both English and French content, taxonomy, menus, and user interface and configuration translations.

*Translation note: User interface and some other items, require being submitted in the **Live** workspace. A notice will be displayed, and you will have the option to submit your changes in **Live** or cancel. It is permitted to choose to submit your changes in **Live** if you are sure of the translations. It is also permitted to add these translations directly in the **Live** workspace in the future.*

1. Login to the [Montreal 2027](https://montreal2027.ca/user/login/google) website.
2. Switch to the `Staging` workspace. For a hotfix, use the `Hotfix` workspace.
3. Add, edit, or delete [content](https://montreal2027.ca/admin/content).
4. Proofread your work.  Ideally, ask someone else to also proofread your work.
5. Until the scheduled release, you make come back to the workspace and revise your work. After switching to the workspace, from the same menu, you may click on `Manage workspace` to see all content that has currently been revised or added in the workspace.
6. During the release window, the Area Head or other authorized team member will publish the content revisions and additions.
