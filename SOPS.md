## Change control
All work, both technical and content, with the exception of updates to the Committee and Staff taxonomy done by the Onboarding Team, will be tracked in [GitHub](https://github.com/Montreal-2027). There are 3 projects within the Montréal 2027 organization: [Tech](https://github.com/orgs/Montreal-2027/projects/1), [Content](https://github.com/orgs/Montreal-2027/projects/2), and [Translations](https://github.com/orgs/Montreal-2027/projects/3). If you are not familiar with how GitHub operates, reach out to an experienced Web Team member.

### Local setup
1. When you first join the Web Team, go to [GitHub](https://github.com), click on `Sign up` and then `Continue with Google`. Log in using your Montréal 2027 email address. Choose a username, and click `Create account >`.
2. Once your account is created, send your GitHub username to the Area Head so you can be invited to the Web Team on GitHub.
3. When you receive the email with the invitation, click on the link to accept the the invitation and join the team. You will be added to the [Montréal 2027 repository](https://github.com/Montreal-2027/montreal2027.ca), which is where all the configuration, custom templates, styling, and configuration is stored.
