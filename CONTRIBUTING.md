# Contributing to the Montréal 2027 website project

Welcome, and thank you for your interest in contributing to the Montréal 2027 website project. Before getting started, all prospective contributors must contact the Web Team at [website@montreal2027.ca](mailto:website@montreal2027.ca) and wait for guidance before proceeding. We are always interested in speaking with individuals that are experienced Drupal developers as well as Frontend Developers. If you are interested in helping us out content entry, we have more information [available](CONTENT.md).

If you are a developer, after emailing the Web Team, we will schedule a brief 15 to 30 minute call with the Area Head to discuss your experience and how it would best fit in with our needs. After the call, if we decide to proceed, the next steps are:

- [ ] Fill out the volunteer form, indicating that you have talked with the Area Head
- [ ] Go to [the membership portal](https://reg.montreal2027.ca), create an account, and agree to the Code of Conduct
- [ ] Once the previous step has been completed, the Area Head will fill out the Onboarding Request Form, asking for you to be assigned to the Web Team
- [ ] After the Onboarding Request Form has been submitted, it will typically take 48 hours for the request to be processed. Once processed, you will receive an email to your personal email address with instructions on how to activate your Montréal 2027 email
- [ ] Once you have been onboarded and you have setup your Montréal 2027 email, you will be asked to go to [Github](https://github.com) and create an account using your new email address, even if you already have a Github account. We want to keep a clear delineation between your work on Montréal 2027 and any other work you have done, or may be doing.  Send your Github username to the Area Head so they can invite you to the Web Team on Github, which will give you access to the sites repository and projects
- [ ] You will be asked for a public SSH key, which will allow you to sync graphic assets and database dumps from the production website
